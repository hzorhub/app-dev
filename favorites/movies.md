# My Favorite Movies or Series

1. **Breaking Bad** - A chemistry teacher turned drug manufacturer slowly loses his moral compass, and the show is a tense study of pride and consequences.
2. **Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse** - A teenager becomes Spider-Man and meets other Spider-People from across the multiverse in a visually bold, funny, and heartfelt animated film.
3. **Stranger Things** - Kids in a small town uncover a supernatural mystery involving a hidden dimension and a government experiment, blending 80s nostalgia with horror and friendship.
4. **The Dark Knight** - Batman faces the Joker, a criminal who wants to prove that anyone can be corrupted, in a crime thriller with a memorable villain.
5. **Attack on Titan** - Humanity survives behind giant walls until a breach exposes the truth about the Titans, in an anime full of tense battles and twists.
